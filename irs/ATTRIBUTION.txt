Impulse responses in irs\openair\ are derived from the OpenAIR library

Changes made for AudioReverb (tools/prepare_irs.py): converted to stereo (B-format decoded to
virtual cardioids at +/-90 degrees, mid/side decoded, or mono decorrelated), direct sound removed
and aligned to t = 0, 40 Hz high-pass, resampled to 44.1 kHz, truncated to 4.2 s with a fade-out,
peak-normalised, saved as 32-bit float WAV.
